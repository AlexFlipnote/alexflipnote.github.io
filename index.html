Better known as AlexFlipnote around the internet. I'm a developer who builds Discord bots, tools and websites, and I've been at it for a decade now, still learning something new along the way.

Most of what I make comes down to one thing: I love optimising. discord.http runs Discord bots over plain HTTP, mothwing builds sites that ship nothing but HTML, and dotenvplus gets by with zero dependencies. If something can be lighter or faster, I'll probably keep rewriting it until it is, yes yes.

The pins on my GitHub show that best. Want to say hi? Find me on Discord or send me an email.
