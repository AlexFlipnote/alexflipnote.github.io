BluePod is an Android app by AlexFlipnote that shows the battery of your wireless earbuds and lets you change their settings. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## The short version

BluePod does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone by itself.

## What stays on your phone

To work, BluePod keeps a small amount of information on your phone only:

- The Bluetooth address of your earbuds, so it can recognise them when they connect
- Your earbuds' battery levels, model, firmware version and settings
- Your choices in the app's settings

This is deleted when you use "Forget buds" in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall it.

## Permissions and why they are needed

- **Nearby devices (Bluetooth):** to find your earbuds, read their battery and change their settings. BluePod does not use Bluetooth to find your location.
- **Notifications:** for the battery notification and low battery alerts, if you turn them on.
- **Display over other apps:** to show the battery card when your earbuds connect, if you turn it on.
- **Foreground service:** to keep the connection to your earbuds open while the app is closed, only if you turn on "Pause when removed" or "Lower music while you talk".

## Debug reports

If you choose "Send debug report" in Settings, BluePod creates a text report with your phone model, Android version, the app's settings and recent app logs, so a problem can be investigated. Your earbuds' Bluetooth address and serial numbers are left out. The report is only sent if you pick where to send it through Android's share menu, and it goes only to the person or app you choose.

## Children

BluePod is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
